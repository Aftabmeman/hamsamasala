Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At Hamsa Masala, we treat our customers' personal privacy with the same sacred reverence as our 100-year-old spice formulas. Read our clear, honest, and strict privacy commitments below.

Our Unconditional Zero-Spam & Zero-Resale Pledge

We never sell, lease, rent, trade, or share your personal name, mobile number, or delivery address with third-party advertisers, data aggregators, or marketing brokers. Your data is used exclusively to pack your royal spices and deliver them safely to your doorstep.

1

Introduction & Our Ethical Code

This Privacy Policy outlines how Hamsa Masala (“we”, “our”, or “the House of Hamsa”) collects, handles, safeguards, and utilizes personal information when you browse our website, place an order through our online checkout, or interact with our official WhatsApp Concierge (+91 91674 35089).

We operate under the fundamental principle that royal hospitality is rooted in unwavering trust. When you invite our spices into your home, you entrust us with personal details such as your physical residence and contact information. We honor that trust with enterprise-grade data hygiene, transparent practices, and strict legal compliance.

2

Information We Collect

We collect only the precise information necessary to provide an authentic, seamless shopping and doorstep delivery experience:

  • Customer Identity: Full Name of the recipient for invoice generation and delivery recipient verification.
  • Contact Credentials: Primary 10-digit Indian Mobile Phone Number, used for WhatsApp order confirmation receipts, courier SMS tracking updates, and delivery boy arrival coordination.
  • Delivery Logistics Information: Flat/House No., Building Name, Street, Landmark, City, State, and 6-digit Pincode to ensure precision dispatch via our logistics partners.
  • Order & Spice Selection: Items ordered, net weights (e.g. 60g, 120g), selected packaging variants, order timestamps, and total transactional values.
  • Payment Mode Preference: Record of whether an order is processed via UPI, Card, Net Banking, or Cash on Delivery (COD). Note: We do not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or bank account passwords on our servers.
3

How We Use Your Information

Every piece of information collected is deployed strictly for purposeful culinary fulfillment:

  • Order Fulfillment & Packing: Directing our warehouse blenders to prepare and fresh-seal your artisanal spices.
  • Doorstep Delivery Logistics: Generating verified airway bills (AWB) and dispatching packages via Delhivery Express.
  • WhatsApp Real-Time Notifications: Transmitting order summaries, real-time courier tracking URLs, and delivery confirmations directly to your personal WhatsApp chat without manual hold-ups.
  • Customer Concierge Support: Assisting you immediately with recipe inquiries, spice shelf-life questions, order modifications, or address updates.
Zero Unsolicited Marketing: We will never inundate your phone with automated robocalls, marketing blasts, or irrelevant promotional spam. Any communication from Hamsa Masala is strictly related to orders you place or inquiries you initiate.
4

Secret Delivery OTP & Doorstep Confidentiality

To eradicate courier theft, parcel misplacement, and fraudulent delivery disputes, Hamsa Masala employs a proprietary Amazon-Style 4-Digit Secret Delivery OTP System:

When your order is created, our backend generates a confidential 4-digit numeric PIN (e.g., 5921). This PIN is never broadcast in public WhatsApp messages or courier labels. It is displayed exclusively on your personal browser confirmation screen and stored within your private device session.

Couriers and delivery agents have zero advance access to this secret OTP. It must be disclosed by you only upon physical receipt and inspection of your spice package. This guarantees that no package can ever be falsely marked as delivered without your explicit doorstep authorization.

5

Data Storage & Encryption Standards

We take security seriously. All network communications between your web browser and our servers are encrypted via industry-standard 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / TLS technology.

Our backend order repository is housed behind authenticated firewalls with strict administrative access control. Only verified store operations personnel have authorized clearance to view shipping addresses for the express purpose of printing dispatch labels.

6

Trusted Service Providers & Disclosures

We share data solely with verified infrastructure partners who uphold equivalent data protection standards:

  • Delhivery Express: Authorized courier logistics provider receiving recipient name, delivery address, and phone number for transit coordination and last-mile delivery.
  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform: Used strictly to transmit direct order confirmation messages triggered by customer consent.
  • Statutory Authorities: We disclose personal data only when strictly mandated by applicable Indian law, court order, or governmental legal proceeding.
7

Your Rights: Data Access & Erasure

As a valued patron of Hamsa Masala, you maintain complete ownership of your personal data:

  • Right to Review: You may request a complete summary of all past orders and stored contact details linked to your phone number at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: If your delivery address, landmark, or mobile number has changed, contact our concierge to update your master profile instantly.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget Me): You have the absolute right to request the complete deletion of your customer record and contact information from our databases once pending deliveries have concluded.
8

Cookies & Device Session Storage

Our website utilizes local storage and minimal session cookies strictly to preserve your cart contents, retain your active shopping bag between page refreshes, and remember your shipping address for effortless future checkouts. We do not run invasive cross-site tracking pixels or third-party behavioral cookies.

Have Questions About Your Privacy?

Our Data Grievance Officer and Royal Customer Concierge are available directly on WhatsApp to resolve any questions or handle data deletion requests immediately.
