Artisanal Spice Quality & Freshness Guarantee

Every batch of Hamsa Masala is crafted following rigorous culinary standards rooted in historic Persian slow stone-grinding traditions:

  • 100% Pure & Whole Spice Sourcing: We utilize exclusively single-origin, hand-selected whole spices. We guarantee zero synthetic food dyes, zero artificial preservatives, zero MSG, and zero anti-caking chemicals.
  • Cold Stone-Ground Process: Spices are slow-crushed at low temperatures (<28°C) to prevent the evaporation of natural volatile essential oils that provide authentic medicinal and aromatic depth.
  • Freshness & Shelf Life: Unopened airtight luxury packs retain peak freshness for 12 months from the packaging date. Once unsealed, we advise storing the blend in an airtight glass jar in a dry, cool pantry away from direct heat to preserve volatile aromas for up to 6 months.

4-Digit Secret Delivery OTP Doorstep Protocol

To guarantee zero theft, eliminate parcel loss, and ensure complete transparency between the customer and delivery partners, Hamsa Masala implements a mandatory Amazon-Style 4-Digit Secret Delivery OTP System:

Crucial Customer Duty: Doorstep OTP Verification

When your order is placed, a private 4-digit numeric code (e.g. 5921) is unlocked on your browser confirmation screen. You must strictly keep this OTP confidential. Do not disclose it over the telephone or in advance to anyone. Provide it to the delivery agent ONLY after they have physically reached your door and handed over your package.

The delivery agent validates this code on the dedicated delivery verification portal (/delivery). If the code matches the backend registry, the order status officially transitions to DELIVERED. If an incorrect OTP is supplied, the agent cannot complete delivery, thereby safeguarding your order against unauthorized handovers.
