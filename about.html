Whispers of Old Isfahan

In the majestic 16th-century royal courts of Isfahan and Tehran, master spice alchemists were revered not merely as cooks, but as artists of aroma and health. Spices were carefully hand-harvested along ancient Silk Road caravans — fragrant wild saffron from Khorasan, fragrant cardamom pods from green mountain slopes, and delicate sun-dried rose petals.

Hamsa Masala was born out of a relentless dedication to preserve these lost artisanal blending secrets. In 1928, our founding blenders began formulating small-batch spice blends inspired by royal banquet menus recorded in ancient Persian culinary manuscripts.

Unlike modern industrialized spice factories that pulverize raw ingredients at extreme friction heat, our method honors the historic slow stone-crush method — preserving delicate volatile essential oils, natural rich pigmentation, and unparalleled lingering fragrance.

Artisanal Hand-Crafted Blends

Every small batch is hand-sorted, slow-roasted over gentle embers, and stone-ground to royal perfection.

Slow Stone-Grinding

Industrial grinders destroy spice aroma with high heat (>70°C). Our slow stone-crush keeps temperatures below 28°C, locking 100% essential oils inside every grain.

The Behruz Persian Spice Philosophy

What sets authentic royal Persian masala apart from everyday curry powders? In Persian cuisine, spices are never meant to overpower the palate with burning chili heat; instead, they create an exquisite, layered symphony of warmth, floral undertones, citrus whispers, and rich savory depth.

Each Hamsa Masala signature blend — from our prized Original Classic Biryani Masala to our aromatic Royal Tikka Kabab Marinade and velvety Shahi Korma Blend — undergoes 18 distinct steps of quality inspection.

We select whole spices from trusted heritage farms: whole green cardamom with plump pods, dried Kashmiri red peppers for natural velvet ruby color without artificial dyes, hand-peeled Sri Lankan cinnamon, and whole black peppercorns bursting with organic piperine.
